Background

I started as a medical writer in late 2022, creating congress content, medical education and organizing symposia for clients like AstraZeneca and Eli Lilly.

I enjoyed the writing, but what I enjoyed most was the client conversations, where I sought to understand the problem first, then craft content that communicated complex science with precision and care.

But the limit of writing, for me, was that I crafted the message but wasn't in the room to deliver it.

Solutions Engineering keeps the craft of communicating complexity but puts me in the room to communicate it live with the customer. A role where I can still draw on technical depth, curiosity first, and telling a story that addresses the client's problems. But in a way that draws on my natural gifts.

To prepare, I built a MEDDIC discovery tool, and have run dozens of mock discovery calls and demos with experienced SE leaders. Now I'm ready to put what I've learned into practice in a live role.
